of two YoutTube creators, the first closest
is "Lord Beatjitzu" (an instrumental) which
was played right next to an omnidirectional
studio microphone, the second creator is
"Mystery Man" which is simultaniously being
played at about 4 feet away from the micro-
phone on a Roku TV.  The volumes are little
attempt to be similar when in the room, but
"Lord Beatjitzu" is predominatly heard by
default when listening to the wave fle play.

selecting "Magic..." from the file menu will
ask for the distance in which you want to
isolate out, or filter the media to.  Enter
4ft and the application will produce a new
WAVE that hears predominatly "Mystery Man."
